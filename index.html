Our Legacy

The Kamaxi Story

Two generations of passion, Entrepreneurship, and authentic Goan hospitality by the sea.

The Raikar Family

Founders & Guardians of Kamaxi

The Foundation of a Dream

Kamaxi Beach Resort began with a singular, heartfelt vision by its founder, Mr. Laxman Raikar. Driven by a deep love for the serene Goan coastline and a desire to share the authentic beauty of these shores with the world, he laid the foundation of what would become a sanctuary for travellers from across India and beyond.

He didn't just build accommodation — he established a philosophy of hospitality rooted in warmth, humility, and the utmost respect for the land. Every decision was guided by a deep reverence for the natural beauty of Polem Beach and the rich traditions of Goan culture.

A New Generation of Entrepreneurship

As the resort flourished, so did the involvement of his son, Mr. Atul Laxman Raikar. Taking the helm with fresh energy and an even deeper sense of responsibility, Atul built upon his father's legacy while evolving Kamaxi into a more complete retreat experience. Under his Entrepreneurship, the resort became not just a place to stay, but a dedicated guardian of this precious stretch of coastline.

Cultivating Natural Beauty

Atul's passion extends far beyond the walls of the resort. He recognised early on that the soul of Kamaxi lies in the pristine sands and the rhythmic tides of Polem Beach. He personally spearheaded initiatives to protect and enhance the beachfront environment:

Shoreline Purity

Rigorous daily cleaning standards to keep Polem's sands spotless and welcoming for all.

Nature First

Preserving the natural flora, fauna, and ecosystems that make this location truly unique.

Sustainable Growth

Growing resort amenities thoughtfully while maintaining the untouched feel of the landscape.

Our Promise to You

Today, Kamaxi Beach Resort stands as a testament to two generations of the Raikar family's dedication. When you stay with us, you aren't just a guest at our resort — you become part of a legacy that values the environment as much as your personal comfort. We invite you to experience the natural beauty that the Raikar family has spent decades nurturing and protecting. Welcome to our home. Welcome to Kamaxi.

Directions & Taxi Services

How to Reach Us

FromGoa International Airport (Dabolim)

Take NH66 South via Margao towards Chaudi / Canacona. Continue to Loliem and follow signs to Polem Beach. The resort is on the beachfront.

~75 km ~1.5 – 2 hrs by road
FromCanacona Railway Station (Chaudi)

Canacona (Chaudi) is the nearest railway station on the Konkan Railway line. From there, take a taxi or auto-rickshaw directly to Polem Beach — a short, easy ride.

~8 km ~15 – 20 min
FromMargao / Panaji by Bus

Take a state or private bus from Margao Bus Stand to Canacona (Chaudi). From Chaudi bus stand, a local taxi or auto-rickshaw will take you directly to Polem Beach.

~60 km from Margao ~1.5 hrs by bus
FromMopa Airport (North Goa)

From the new Mopa Airport in North Goa, take NH66 South all the way to Canacona. Continue to Loliem / Polem Village and follow signs to Polem Beach.

~140 km ~2.5 – 3 hrs by road

Cancellation Policy

Strict Policy

Kamakshi Beach Resort follows a strict cancellation policy.

Once a reservation has been confirmed and the advance payment has been received, the advance payment is non-refundable.

In the event of cancellation, the guest will not be entitled to a refund of the amount paid.

The reservation and any amount paid towards it are not transferable to another person.

However, where a guest provides sufficient advance notice, Kamakshi Beach Resort may, at its discretion, allow the guest to change the dates of their reservation, subject to the conditions set out in the Date Change Policy below.

Important: Last-minute cancellation or date-change requests, including requests made only one or two days before arrival, will generally not be accepted.
03

Date Change Policy

Modifications

Kamakshi Beach Resort may, at its discretion, permit one date change only for a confirmed reservation, provided the request is made sufficiently in advance and the requested dates are available.

Only one date change will be permitted per reservation.
The revised dates must fall within the same season as the original reservation.
The revised stay must be completed within the applicable season, with the current season ending on 31 May.
The revised dates are subject to room availability.
The advance payment will remain credited against the revised reservation and will not be refunded.
The reservation cannot be transferred to another guest.
If the room rate for the revised dates is higher, the guest must pay the difference in rate.
If the room rate for the revised dates is lower, no refund or credit will be provided for the difference.
Once a date change has been made, no further date changes will be permitted.
04

No-Show Policy

Arrivals

If a guest does not arrive on the scheduled check-in date and has not informed Kamakshi Beach Resort of a delay, the reservation will be treated as a No-Show.

No refund or date change will be provided for a No-Show.

The reserved room will normally be held until check-out time on the following morning, after which the reservation may be treated as cancelled and released for other guests.

05

Early Check-Out

Departures

If a guest chooses to check out before the confirmed departure date for personal reasons, no refund will be provided for the unused nights.

The guest remains responsible for the full amount applicable to the confirmed reservation duration.

06

If suitable alternative accommodation cannot be provided, Kamakshi Beach Resort will refund the amount paid for any accommodation that the Resort is unable to provide.

Any refund will be made to the original payment method and may take several working days to appear, depending on the bank or payment provider.
